Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

GTA Clipper (“the app”) is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to publish video clips to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google API Services): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the name of the connected channel (youtube.readonly), so the owner can see which channel is connected.
  • Instagram / Facebook: the list of the owner's Facebook Pages and the Instagram professional account linked to them, and permission to publish Reels to that account.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name) and permission to send videos to the account's drafts (inbox).

The app does not read private messages, comments, contacts, viewer data or analytics, and does not access any account other than the ones its owner connects.

How the data is used and stored

  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a folder readable only by the owner's Windows account.
  • The data is used only to upload the owner's clips and to show which account is connected.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app has no servers: nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform's own official API.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove “gta-clipper”.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove “gta-clipper”.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services → remove the app.

After access is revoked the stored tokens stop working; the owner deletes them with the app's auth --remove command. To request deletion of any data, contact the owner below — the app stores no data about anyone other than its owner.

Политика конфиденциальности

GTA Clipper — личная программа владельца, которая публикует его клипы на его же аккаунты YouTube, Instagram и TikTok.

  • Программа получает только право загружать видео и видеть название подключённого аккаунта (в TikTok — класть видео в черновики).
  • Ключи доступа хранятся только на компьютере владельца. Никуда, кроме официальных интерфейсов площадок, данные не уходят; они не продаются, не передаются третьим лицам и не используются для рекламы или обучения моделей.
  • Сообщения, комментарии, контакты и данные зрителей программа не читает.
  • Отозвать доступ: в настройках аккаунта Google, Facebook или TikTok удалить приложение «gta-clipper». Связаться: github.com/molyrekt.
